Everything you copy, on a line.

Screenshots, recordings, text and links hang just under the GNOME top bar. Click one to copy it again, or drag it into any app.

curl -fsSL https://dabane-ghassan.github.io/festoon/install.sh | sh

Paste it in a terminal, then log out and back in once. Version 1.0.2, free and open source, for GNOME 45 or later on any distro.

📸

Screenshots

Every screenshot hangs in a glass frame the moment you take it. So do screen recordings.

📋

Copies

Text becomes a note, web addresses a link, pictures a picture. Never what a password manager copies.

🫳

Drag

Drag an item into a chat, an email or a folder. Click to copy it, hold to mark it up.

🔎

Search

Finds notes, links and the words inside your screenshots, read on your computer.

Good to know

  • Nothing leaves your computer. No account, no network, no analytics. Items live in ~/.local/share/festoon.
  • Out of your way. While the line is up, every click goes to the window underneath.
  • Scriptable. festoon hang shot.png, festoon copy 2, echo hi | festoon hang -, and D-Bus.
  • Optional: install tesseract and search reads the text in screenshots.

A GNOME take on Tendedero for the Mac. From the maker of Manicule.
